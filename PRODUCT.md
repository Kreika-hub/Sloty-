# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary user: Admin (building manager who handles day-to-day parking operations).
Secondary users: Guard (security personnel registering vehicle movements), Resident (tenants checking spots or making payments), and Master (system-level superadmin).
Context: Residential buildings with managed parking in Caracas, Venezuela, designed to scale across the country and internationally (multi-building, multi-city architecture).

## Product Purpose
Sloty digitizes and centralizes parking management in residential buildings, replacing logbooks, Excel spreadsheets, and WhatsApp. It handles access control, spot assignment, membership payments, and movement traceability. Success means providing an agile, fast, and frictionless tool—a top-tier, reliable app experience—that makes stakeholders feel accompanied and supported every day without crashes or data loss.

## Positioning
Sloty replaces the notebook, Excel, and WhatsApp group with a single real-time system where every vehicle movement, payment, and alert is recorded, visible, and synchronized across guards, admins, residents, and masters, with no double work or lost information.

## Brand Personality
Agile, clear, and modern. It feels like Linear (clean transitions, speed), Revolut/Nubank (fintech-level trust and clarity), and Notion (order and visual hierarchy). Features smooth micro-interactions, immediate visual/tactile feedback on actions, and an alive, fluid interface rather than a static one.

## Anti-references
Should not look like a gray, dense, or intimidating corporate/government legacy access control software from the 2010s. Avoid overloaded screens without hierarchy, long unguided forms, technical/cryptic error messages, static interfaces without feedback, or any feeling of an abandoned, unresponsive system.

## Design Principles
- Uncompromised clarity: Never sacrifice clarity for space; organize complex data into logical submenus or folders.
- Companion, not a machine: Handle loading, empty, and error states gracefully. Errors should guide and reassure, never blame or convey brokenness.
- Predictable and responsive: Provide immediate visual/haptic feedback for every action so users, especially guards in a hurry, never doubt if their input registered.
- Professional fluidity: Utilize smooth, purposeful micro-animations and transitions to make the experience feel top-tier and alive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Optimized for long shifts, direct sunlight, and nighttime usage in a fast-paced environment. Requires high contrast across all states with the dark/gold theme, large tap targets (generous text and buttons) for use with gloves or in a hurry, and avoidance of visual fatigue (no flickering, excessive animations, or low-contrast secondary text).
